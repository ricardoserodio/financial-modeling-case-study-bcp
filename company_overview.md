# Company Overview

## Company

**Millennium bcp / Banco Comercial Português** is a Portuguese listed banking group headquartered in Portugal.

The bank operates primarily in the Portuguese banking market and also has relevant international operations, including Poland through Bank Millennium and Mozambique through Millennium bim. The Group also has exposure to Angola through Banco Millennium Atlântico, recognised through equity-accounted earnings.

Millennium bcp is listed on Euronext Lisbon and publishes annual reports, investor presentations, financial statements and market disclosures, making it suitable for a public-source financial modeling case study.

---

## Why This Company Was Selected

Millennium bcp was selected for this project because it is one of the most relevant listed banks in Portugal and provides sufficient public information for a structured banking analysis.

The bank is appropriate for this case study because it allows analysis of:

- banking profitability;
- net interest income;
- fees and commissions;
- operating efficiency;
- impairments and provisions;
- credit quality;
- capital ratios;
- loans and deposits;
- valuation multiples;
- scenario analysis;
- public-source financial data validation.

As a listed bank, Millennium bcp also provides market data that can be used for educational valuation analysis, including price-to-book and price-to-earnings logic.

## Business Model

Millennium bcp operates as a universal banking group.

Its business model is mainly based on:

- lending to individuals, companies and institutions;
- attracting customer deposits;
- generating net interest income from the difference between interest earned on assets and interest paid on liabilities;
- earning fees and commissions from banking services, cards, payments, asset management, insurance distribution and other financial products;
- managing credit risk, liquidity risk, capital requirements and operating efficiency.

For this reason, the project uses a bank-specific financial model rather than a traditional industrial company model.

## Relevance for Financial Modeling

A bank financial model differs from a standard industrial company model.

For this project, the model focuses on:

- net interest income instead of product revenue;
- fees and commissions as non-interest income;
- operating costs and cost-to-income ratio;
- impairments and cost of risk;
- loans and deposits instead of inventories and trade receivables;
- regulatory capital ratios;
- valuation using banking multiples such as P/B and P/E.

This makes the project particularly relevant for banking analysis, financial research, data validation and AI finance evaluation tasks.

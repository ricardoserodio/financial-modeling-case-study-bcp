# Dashboard Structure

This document defines the planned dashboard structure for the **Financial Modeling Case Study – Millennium bcp / Portuguese Listed Bank** project.

The dashboard is designed as an executive-style banking analytics report built from public financial data.

This project is for educational and portfolio purposes only.

It does not constitute investment advice.

---

## Dashboard Objective

The objective of the dashboard is to transform structured public financial data into clear, visual and interpretable banking insights.

The dashboard should help answer:

- How has the bank performed historically?
- What are the main profitability trends?
- Is operating efficiency improving?
- How is asset quality evolving?
- Is the capital position stable?
- How do forecasts compare with historical figures?
- What is the current data quality status?
- Which figures still require validation?

---

## Dashboard Audience

The dashboard should be understandable for:

- financial analysts;
- banking professionals;
- data analysts;
- reporting analysts;
- recruiters;
- business operations stakeholders;
- non-technical decision-makers.

The dashboard should use clear labels, simple explanations and neutral commentary.

## Page 1 – Executive Summary

### Purpose

Provide a high-level overview of the most important banking KPIs.

### Suggested Visuals

- KPI cards
- Small trend charts
- Summary table
- Data quality status indicator
- Short commentary box

### Suggested KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Net income | Shows final profitability |
| Net interest income | Shows core banking income from lending and deposits |
| Fees and commissions | Shows recurring non-interest income |
| Cost-to-income ratio | Shows operating efficiency |
| ROE | Shows profitability relative to equity |
| ROA | Shows profitability relative to assets |
| CET1 ratio | Shows capital strength |
| NPL ratio | Shows asset quality |
| Customer loans | Shows lending activity |
| Customer deposits | Shows funding base |

### Suggested Commentary

The commentary should summarise historical performance in a neutral and factual way.

Example wording:

    The bank recorded changes in profitability, efficiency and capital indicators across the analysed period. Further interpretation depends on validated public-source data and should be read together with the data quality status.

## Page 2 – Profitability

### Purpose

Analyse income generation, net income and return metrics.

### Suggested Visuals

- Net income line chart
- Net interest income line chart
- Fees and commissions bar chart
- ROE and ROA trend chart
- Income mix chart

### Suggested KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Net income | Final profit after costs, impairments and tax |
| Net interest income | Main income source for most banks |
| Fees and commissions | Diversified revenue source |
| Operating income | Total recurring banking income |
| ROE | Return generated on shareholder equity |
| ROA | Return generated on total assets |

### Analytical Questions

- Is net income increasing or decreasing?
- Is net interest income the main driver of performance?
- Are fees and commissions stable?
- Is ROE improving?
- Is ROA consistent with profitability trends?

### Suggested Commentary

    Profitability should be interpreted together with interest rate context, cost discipline, impairments and capital levels. A higher net income figure is positive only if supported by sustainable income quality and controlled risk.

---

## Page 3 – Efficiency

### Purpose

Analyse cost control and operating efficiency.

### Suggested Visuals

- Cost-to-income ratio line chart
- Operating income vs operating costs chart
- Operating cost growth chart
- Efficiency KPI cards

### Suggested KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Operating income | Revenue base available to cover costs and risks |
| Operating costs | Cost base required to run the bank |
| Cost-to-income ratio | Efficiency measure |
| Operating cost growth | Shows cost pressure |
| Operating income growth | Shows revenue expansion |

### Analytical Questions

- Are operating costs growing faster than income?
- Is the cost-to-income ratio improving?
- Is the bank generating operating leverage?
- Are efficiency gains visible over time?

### Suggested Commentary

    Cost-to-income is a key banking efficiency indicator. A lower ratio generally indicates better efficiency, but interpretation should consider inflation, restructuring costs, business growth and one-off effects.

---

## Page 4 – Asset Quality

### Purpose

Analyse credit risk indicators and loan book quality.

### Suggested Visuals

- NPL ratio trend
- Cost of risk trend
- Impairments and provisions trend
- Customer loans trend
- Asset quality summary table

### Suggested KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| NPL ratio | Indicates proportion of problematic loans |
| Cost of risk | Measures credit risk cost relative to loan book |
| Impairments and provisions | Shows expected or recognised credit losses |
| Customer loans | Shows size of lending book |
| Coverage ratio | Shows provision coverage of problematic assets, if available |

### Analytical Questions

- Is the NPL ratio improving or deteriorating?
- Are credit risk costs increasing?
- Are impairments aligned with loan book evolution?
- Does loan growth appear controlled from a risk perspective?

### Suggested Commentary

    Asset quality is central in banking analysis because credit losses can materially affect profitability and capital. NPL ratio and cost of risk should be interpreted together rather than in isolation.

---

## Page 5 – Capital & Liquidity

### Purpose

Analyse capital strength, balance sheet structure and funding indicators.

### Suggested Visuals

- CET1 ratio trend
- Customer loans vs customer deposits chart
- Loan-to-deposit ratio trend
- Equity and total assets chart
- Capital KPI cards

### Suggested KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| CET1 ratio | Core capital strength indicator |
| Total capital ratio | Broader capital adequacy measure, if available |
| Customer loans | Lending activity |
| Customer deposits | Stable customer funding base |
| Loan-to-deposit ratio | Relationship between lending and customer funding |
| Equity | Accounting capital base |
| Total assets | Balance sheet size |

### Analytical Questions

- Is the CET1 ratio stable?
- Are loans and deposits growing consistently?
- Is lending funded primarily by customer deposits?
- Is the capital position resilient across the analysed period?

### Suggested Commentary

    Capital ratios are especially important for banks because they indicate the ability to absorb losses and meet regulatory requirements. Public analysis should remain cautious and avoid conclusions beyond the available data.

---

## Page 6 – Forecast & Scenarios

### Purpose

Compare historical actuals with simplified forecast estimates.

### Suggested Visuals

- Actual vs forecast trend chart
- Scenario comparison table
- Base vs downside vs upside chart
- Forecast assumptions table
- Sensitivity summary

### Suggested KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Net income forecast | Shows projected profitability under assumptions |
| ROE forecast | Shows expected return profile under scenario logic |
| Cost-to-income forecast | Shows expected efficiency path |
| Cost of risk forecast | Shows credit risk assumption |
| CET1 forecast | Shows capital assumption |
| Customer loans forecast | Shows lending growth assumption |
| Customer deposits forecast | Shows funding growth assumption |

### Analytical Questions

- How do 2026E, 2027E and 2028E compare with 2025A?
- Which assumptions drive the base scenario?
- What changes in the downside scenario?
- Which metrics are most sensitive to assumptions?
- Are assumptions clearly separated from actual data?

### Suggested Commentary

    Forecast values are simplified educational estimates based on documented assumptions. They should not be interpreted as predictions, investment advice or recommendations.

---

## Page 7 – Data Quality

### Purpose

Show the reliability, completeness and validation status of the project data.

### Suggested Visuals

- Validation status count
- Missing values count
- Source coverage table
- Reported vs calculated classification
- Actual vs forecast classification
- Data quality scorecard
- Items requiring review table

### Suggested KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Validated items | Number of data points validated against public sources |
| Pending items | Number of data points still requiring review |
| Missing values | Number of incomplete data points |
| Items needing review | Number of issues requiring manual checking |
| Source coverage | Percentage of data points mapped to a public source |
| Actual vs forecast split | Shows classification of real data vs estimates |

### Analytical Questions

- Are all key figures mapped to public sources?
- Which data points are still pending?
- Are there missing values?
- Are actual and forecast values clearly separated?
- Are reported and calculated figures clearly classified?
- Which items require human review?

### Suggested Commentary

    Data quality is part of the analytical output. A dashboard should not only show financial metrics, but also whether the underlying data has been validated and properly sourced.

- readable charts;
- clear units;
- short commentary boxes;

- no unnecessary decoration;

## Suggested Data Model

The dashboard can be based on the following tables:

| Table | Usage |
|---|---|
| `financial_data` | Main metrics by period |
| `banking_ratios` | Banking ratio trends |
| `forecast_assumptions` | Forecast assumptions |
| `scenario_analysis` | Scenario outputs |
| `source_mapping` | Source traceability |
| `data_quality_checks` | Data validation results |

Recommended simple relationships:

| From | To | Relationship Logic |
|---|---|---|
| `financial_data[period]` | `banking_ratios[period]` | Period-level comparison |
| `financial_data[metric]` | `source_mapping[data_item]` | Source traceability |
| `scenario_analysis[period]` | `forecast_assumptions[period]` | Forecast period alignment |
| `scenario_analysis[scenario]` | `forecast_assumptions[scenario]` | Scenario alignment |

The model should remain simple, transparent and easy to explain.

## Suggested DAX Measures

Possible DAX measures:

    Total Value = SUM(financial_data[value])

    Validated Items =
    COUNTROWS(
        FILTER(
            source_mapping,
            source_mapping[validation_status] = "Validated"
        )
    )

    Pending Items =
    COUNTROWS(
        FILTER(
            source_mapping,
            source_mapping[validation_status] = "Pending"
        )
    )

    Missing Values =
    COUNTROWS(
        FILTER(
            financial_data,
            ISBLANK(financial_data[value])
        )
    )

    Forecast Items =
    COUNTROWS(
        FILTER(
            financial_data,
            financial_data[data_type] = "Forecast"
        )
    )

    Actual Items =
    COUNTROWS(
        FILTER(
            financial_data,
            financial_data[data_type] = "Actual"
        )
    )

These measures should be reviewed once the final data structure is confirmed.

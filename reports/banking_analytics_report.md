# Banking Analytics Report – Millennium bcp Case Study

## Purpose of This Report

This report presents an initial banking analytics review of Millennium bcp using only publicly available information.

The objective is to demonstrate a structured approach to:

- banking ratio analysis;
- public financial data extraction;
- financial statement interpretation;
- source validation;
- data quality awareness;
- SQL and Power BI reporting preparation;
- human-in-the-loop analytical review.

This report is part of a professional portfolio project and is intended for educational, analytical and career development purposes only.

It does not provide investment advice, a price target, a buy/sell/hold recommendation or any form of financial recommendation.

## Initial 2025A Findings

### Profitability

Millennium bcp reported net income of EUR 1,018.6 million in 2025A.

The bank reported:

| Metric | 2025A |
|---|---:|
| ROE | 14.1% |
| ROA | 1.1% |
| Net interest margin | 2.89% |
| EPS | EUR 0.066 |
| Book value per share | EUR 0.528 |

These indicators suggest a strong profitability profile in 2025A.

However, profitability should not be assessed in isolation. A full analytical review requires comparison with prior years, peer banks, cost of risk, capital development, interest rate conditions and balance sheet growth.

---

### Revenue and Efficiency

Millennium bcp reported:

| Metric | 2025A |
|---|---:|
| Net interest income | EUR 2,898.1 million |
| Operating income | EUR 3,815.2 million |
| Operating costs | EUR 1,415.1 million |
| Cost-to-income ratio | 37.1% |
| Cost-to-income ratio excluding specific items | 36.5% |

The cost-to-income ratio indicates a relatively efficient operating structure.

From a banking analytics perspective, this ratio is important because it links revenue generation with the cost base required to produce that revenue.

A lower cost-to-income ratio generally suggests higher operating efficiency, but it must be interpreted carefully. Efficiency can be influenced by revenue mix, interest rate environment, restructuring effects, inflation, digital investment, staff costs and one-off items.

### Asset Quality

Millennium bcp reported the following asset quality indicators for 2025A:

| Metric | 2025A |
|---|---:|
| Cost of risk | 32 bps |
| NPE ratio | 2.4% |
| NPE coverage ratio | 90.9% |
| Restructured loans ratio | 1.8% |

These indicators are relevant for assessing the quality of the loan book and the level of provisioning coverage.

The NPE ratio provides insight into the level of non-performing exposures relative to the bank's credit exposure.

The NPE coverage ratio indicates the extent to which non-performing exposures are covered by impairments or related coverage mechanisms.

A full review should validate whether NPE, NPL and impairment terminology is used consistently across reporting periods.

## Initial 2024A–2025A Comparison

This section provides an initial year-on-year comparison between the official 2024A reexpressed figures and the official 2025A figures extracted from Millennium bcp public sources.

The purpose is not to provide investment advice, a price target or a buy/sell recommendation. The objective is to demonstrate structured public-data analysis, banking ratio interpretation, source validation and human-in-the-loop analytical commentary.

---

### Profitability

Millennium bcp reported an increase in net income from EUR 906.4 million in 2024A to EUR 1,018.6 million in 2025A.

| Metric | 2024A | 2025A | Direction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Net income | EUR 906.4 million | EUR 1,018.6 million | Increase |
| ROE | 13.8% | 14.1% | Increase |
| ROA | 1.0% | 1.1% | Increase |
| EPS | EUR 0.058 | EUR 0.066 | Increase |
| Book value per share | EUR 0.469 | EUR 0.528 | Increase |

Return on equity increased slightly from 13.8% in 2024A to 14.1% in 2025A, while return on assets increased from 1.0% to 1.1%.

This suggests that the bank maintained a strong profitability profile in 2025, with net income growth translating into a modest improvement in profitability ratios.

However, these figures should be interpreted together with balance sheet growth, interest rate environment, impairment levels, capital development and distribution policy.
